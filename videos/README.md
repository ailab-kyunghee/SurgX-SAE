# Explanation videos

Ten explanation videos, four for Causal ASFormer × Cholec80 (`CA-Ch`) and two for each of Causal ASFormer × AutoLaparo (`CA-AL`), TeCNO × Cholec80 (`Te-Ch`) and TeCNO × AutoLaparo (`Te-AL`).
Each video covers 60 s of a held-out test video at 1 fps, played at four times the original speed, and shows the prediction, the ground truth and the explanations of both methods for every frame.
The file name gives the setting, the test video and the time of the central frame in seconds.

The frames are from Cholec80 and AutoLaparo and remain subject to the terms of these datasets.
